The dark

Your lantern burns oil. As it runs low your light shrinks and flickers. Without light your sanity falls fast. Refill with Lantern Oil.

Low sanity brings whispers, distortion and things that aren't really there. At zero, madness eats your life. Braziers, shrines and Calming Tonics restore your mind.

The Still only moves when you are not looking at it. Pale Stalkers appear behind you. Watch the edges of your light.

Loot

Common · Magic · Rare · Epic · Legendary · Cursed. Better items roll more affixes with higher tiers (◆) as you go deeper. Legendaries change how you play. Cursed items carry a dark gift and a price.

Salvage unwanted gear into dust. The Pale Peddler can reforge (reroll) or imbue (raise rarity) items for dust. Elites carry Bone Keys to locked vaults. Some walls are cracked. Hit them.

Dying keeps your Ashes. Spend them at the Ember Hearth.
